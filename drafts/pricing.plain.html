Frequently asked questions

Can I try Frame.io for free?

Yes! Our Free plan gives you access to core features with up to 2 members and 2 GB of storage. You can also start a free trial of our Pro or Team plans to experience the full platform.

What happens when my trial ends?

When your trial ends, you can continue using Frame.io on the Free plan, or upgrade to a paid plan to keep access to premium features and increased storage.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes, you can switch between billing cycles at any time. When switching to annual billing, you'll save 13% compared to monthly billing.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and can arrange invoicing for Enterprise plans.

How does storage work?

Each plan includes a base amount of storage. Pro and Team plans include additional storage per member added. Enterprise plans offer custom storage configurations.
